Elevator Financial Services — Office Guide: Dubai
Last updated: April 2026

=== OFFICE OVERVIEW ===

Location: Gate District, Dubai International Financial Centre (DIFC), Dubai, UAE
Time zone: GST (UTC+4) — UAE does not observe daylight saving time
Regional head: Middle East and Africa

The Dubai office covers Elevator Financial Services' Middle East and Africa client relationships and acts as a regional delivery hub for MEA-based engagements. Its location within the DIFC gives it a regulatory environment aligned with international financial services standards.

=== WORKING HOURS ===

Core hours: 09:00 to 17:30 GST, Monday to Friday
The UAE moved to a Monday to Friday working week in January 2022. The Dubai office operates fully on this schedule.
The Dubai office observes UAE public holidays as announced by the UAE government, typically confirmed each year in advance but subject to change based on moon sighting for Islamic holidays.

=== MEETING PREFERENCES ===

- Thursday afternoons (after 14:00 GST) are treated as a wind-down period for the week. Avoid scheduling cross-regional or client-facing meetings in this window.
- Friday is a full non-working day. Do not schedule meetings on Fridays for Dubai participants.
- Prayer times fall five times daily. While the Dubai team manages their own schedules around this, it is good practice to avoid scheduling calls at known prayer times, particularly Dhuhr (midday, approximately 12:15–12:45 GST) and Asr (mid-afternoon, approximately 15:30–16:00 GST). Exact times shift daily.
- The preferred window for cross-regional calls involving Dubai and London is 09:00–13:00 GST (05:00–09:00 GMT). Morning slots from London work well for Dubai.
- Ramadan significantly affects working patterns. The Dubai office operates on reduced hours during Ramadan, typically 09:00 to 14:30 GST. Schedule all meetings within this window during Ramadan. Dates vary by year — check the Islamic calendar for the current year's Ramadan period.

=== TIME ZONE CONSIDERATIONS ===

When scheduling with other Elevator offices:
- London: 3–4 hours behind GST depending on BST. Good overlap: 09:00–13:00 GST.
- Warsaw: 2–3 hours behind GST. Good overlap: 09:00–14:00 GST.
- Singapore: 4 hours ahead of GST. Good overlap: 09:00–13:30 GST.
- New York: 8–9 hours behind GST. Very limited overlap. 09:00 GST = 00:00–01:00 EST. Async strongly preferred.
- Sydney: 6–7 hours ahead of GST. Limited overlap: 09:00–11:00 GST aligns with late afternoon Sydney.

=== PUBLIC HOLIDAYS ===

UAE public holidays include (dates for Islamic holidays vary by year and moon sighting):
- New Year's Day (1 January)
- Eid Al Fitr (3 days — end of Ramadan)
- Arafat Day and Eid Al Adha (3 days)
- Islamic New Year (Hijri New Year)
- Prophet Muhammad's Birthday (Al Mawlid Al Nabawi)
- Commemoration Day (1 December)
- National Day (2–3 December)

Islamic holiday dates are subject to change with one to two days' notice based on moon sighting. Always build flexibility into scheduling around these periods.

=== CULTURAL AND PRACTICAL NOTES ===

- The Dubai office has a diverse international workforce. Sensitivity to Islamic cultural norms is expected from all Elevator staff when scheduling or attending meetings involving Dubai colleagues.
- During Ramadan, avoid scheduling working lunches or food-related social meetings that include Dubai colleagues.
- The summer months (June to August) see reduced in-person attendance in Dubai due to extreme heat and the holiday preferences of internationally mobile staff. Expect higher rates of remote attendance during this period.
- DIFC operates its own regulatory framework. Compliance and legal reviews scheduled with the Dubai team should account for DIFC-specific requirements, which may differ from UK FCA or US SEC requirements.
- Business dress and conduct standards in client-facing settings are formal. This applies to video calls with external clients as well as in-person meetings.
