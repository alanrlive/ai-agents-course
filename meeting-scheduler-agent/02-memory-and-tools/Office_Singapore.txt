Elevator Financial Services — Office Guide: Singapore
Last updated: April 2026

=== OFFICE OVERVIEW ===

Location: One Raffles Quay, North Tower, Singapore 048583
Time zone: SGT (UTC+8) — Singapore does not observe daylight saving time
Regional head: Asia-Pacific

The Singapore office serves as Elevator Financial Services' Asia-Pacific hub. It covers APAC client relationships, regional delivery coordination, and acts as the primary connection point between the firm's European and Australian operations.

=== WORKING HOURS ===

Core hours: 09:00 to 18:00 SGT, Monday to Friday
The Singapore office observes Singapore public holidays as gazetted by the Ministry of Manpower each year.
Late evening calls (after 19:00 SGT) are not expected of Singapore staff unless pre-agreed for critical delivery situations.

=== MEETING PREFERENCES ===

- The Singapore team is the most time-zone constrained office in the Elevator network. When scheduling cross-regional calls, Singapore's window must be considered first, as it has the least flexibility.
- The preferred window for cross-regional calls involving Singapore and EMEA is 08:00–10:00 SGT (00:00–02:00 GMT / 01:00–03:00 CET). This requires early starts from London or Warsaw. These calls should be rotated so Singapore does not always take the late slot.
- For calls involving Singapore and New York only, there is no practical same-day overlap. Use async communication or accept that one party will be outside core hours.
- Singapore public holidays often fall mid-week. Always verify before scheduling.

=== TIME ZONE CONSIDERATIONS ===

When scheduling with other Elevator offices:
- London: 7–8 hours behind SGT depending on BST. Overlap: 08:00–10:00 SGT (start of Singapore day, end of London previous day — check carefully).
- Warsaw: 6–7 hours behind SGT. Similar constraints to London.
- Dubai: 4 hours behind SGT. Good overlap: 09:00–14:00 SGT aligns with Dubai core hours.
- Sydney: 2–3 hours ahead of SGT depending on AEDT. Good overlap: 09:00–15:00 SGT.
- New York: 12–13 hours behind SGT. No practical overlap. Async preferred.

=== PUBLIC HOLIDAYS ===

Singapore observes the following public holidays (dates vary by year):
- New Year's Day (1 January)
- Chinese New Year (2 days — dates vary, typically January or February)
- Good Friday
- Hari Raya Puasa (dates vary)
- Labour Day (1 May)
- Vesak Day (date varies)
- Hari Raya Haji (dates vary)
- National Day (9 August)
- Deepavali (date varies)
- Christmas Day (25 December)

Note: Chinese New Year is a significant period of reduced availability. Many Singapore-based staff take extended leave around this period. Check with the Singapore office manager before scheduling meetings in the two weeks surrounding Chinese New Year.

=== CULTURAL AND PRACTICAL NOTES ===

- Singapore has a multicultural workforce. Be aware that different religious observances affect individual availability. This is especially relevant during Ramadan, Chinese New Year, and Deepavali periods.
- The Singapore office values punctuality. Starting meetings late reflects poorly on the organiser.
- Air travel between Singapore and European offices is long (12–14 hours). When scheduling in-person meetings requiring travel from EMEA, allow at least one recovery day before expecting the traveller to present or lead sessions.
- August is Singapore's National Day month. The office is festive but fully operational apart from the public holiday on 9 August.
